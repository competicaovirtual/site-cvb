What we do

Four fronts.
One arena.

GP Brasil CVB · Tour Parque da Cidade
01

Virtual competitions

From rulebook to podium.

We plan and run complete virtual races, online (each athlete on their own trainer or rowing machine) or in person, with a full arena and a live audience.

  • Real-world routes on the ROUVY and EXR platforms
  • Registration, heats, categories and rankings
  • Officiating and result validation
  • Live streaming with commentary and giant screen
  • Real-world route recording for ROUVY
02

Live brand activations

Your brand at the center of the arena.

We bring smart bikes and rowing machines, a giant screen and our crew to trade shows, malls, races and corporate events. People ride, row, compete and take your brand home with them.

  • Arena with simulators and LED screen
  • Quick challenges with a live leaderboard
  • Your brand's route and visual identity
  • Lead capture and social media content
  • Setup, operation and teardown by our crew
03

Results systems

In-house development, tailored to your event.

We build results and ranking systems for virtual competitions and marketing activations, branded for your event and accessible on any phone.

  • Results by heat, category and country
  • Result validation via screenshots
  • Annual and stage rankings
  • Live leaderboard display for activations
  • Participant registration and admin area
Live stream · Virtual L'Étape Brasil
04

Live streaming

Every pedal stroke on screen and online.

A live-streaming system for virtual competitions: we combine the athletes' screens, the route and the leaderboard into a single broadcast for the venue's giant screen and the internet.

  • Several athletes on screen at once
  • Leaderboard, power and positions on screen
  • Live commentary
  • Streaming on YouTube and social media
  • LED screen at the venue

How it works

From idea to finish line.

  1. 01

    Briefing

    We understand your goal: official competition, brand activation, corporate event, or all of the above.

  2. 02

    Setup

    We define the route, format and equipment, and build the arena with platform, screen and sound.

  3. 03

    Live

    Start, commentary, real-time rankings and an engaged crowd from the first to the last meter.

  4. 04

    Results

    Validated results in our system, recorded stream, photos and data for your brand to use after the event.
